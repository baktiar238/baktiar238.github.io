About

Hey! I am Baktiar Wasir Farooq. Welcome to my personal website.

I am a fourth-year B.Sc. (Hons.) with Research student at Kirori Mal College, University of Delhi, and one of the founding members of the KMC Neutrino Research Group, where I am advised by Prof. Bipin Singh Koranga.

My current research is centered on quantum information theory and its intersection with particle physics, more precisely neutrino physics, with particular emphasis on entanglement theory, quantum correlations and Fisher information analysis.

My ongoing work studies triality relations for the wave-packet model of neutrinos. I am using open data from MINOS and have made predictions for DUNE; this is my final-year bachelor's thesis project. I am also working on neutrino tomography with a focus on symmetry-violation studies.

Contact: baktiarfarooq238@gmail.com

Research interests

I am broadly interested in how quantum-information concepts and wave-packet physics shape what neutrino experiments can measure.

Neutrino oscillation phenomenology

Three-flavor and sterile-neutrino oscillations, matter effects, and long-baseline analysis for MINOS and DUNE.

Quantum information in oscillations

Entanglement entropy, wave-particle-entanglement complementarity, and quantum and classical Fisher information.

Wave-packet treatments

Wave-packet width, decoherence models, and what long-baseline data can say about quantum foundations.

Computation and data analysis

Python tools for oscillation analysis, and a growing focus on experimental data analysis.

Internships & research projects

My research spans neutrino physics, quantum information theory, quantum entanglement, and computational studies of neutrino oscillations. Through projects, internships and international collaborations, I have explored wave-packet effects, quantum-information measures, matter effects and neutrino phenomenology, connecting theory with numerical simulation and experimental data.

2025 – Present

International Research Project

Università degli Studi di Salerno (UNISA), Italy

External supervisor: Prof. Massimo Blasone
Internal supervisor: Prof. Bipin Singh Koranga

Project: Probing Quantum Foundations in Long-Baseline Neutrino Oscillations: Wave Packet Approach from MINOS Data to DUNE Predictions.

I have been investigating the wave-packet description of long-baseline neutrino oscillations, focusing on the connection between quantum coherence, neutrino propagation and observable oscillation probabilities.

  • Derived wave-packet oscillation probabilities from first principles and fitted the framework to MINOS data to investigate the low-energy discrepancy.
  • Extended the analysis to DUNE, incorporating realistic matter effects and neutrino mass ordering.
  • Used Fisher information to identify regions where the neutrino wave-packet spatial width is most strongly constrained.
  • Explored the triality relation between probability, visibility and entanglement (P², V², E²) for detectors sensitive to muon-neutrino states.

Research focus: Neutrino oscillations, wave-packet coherence, MINOS data analysis, DUNE predictions, quantum foundations, Fisher information.

2024 – Present

Undergraduate Research Assistant

Kirori Mal College, University of Delhi, India

Supervisor: Prof. Bipin Singh Koranga

My research at KMC focuses on the intersection of neutrino physics and quantum information theory, using entanglement and quantum-information measures to study neutrino oscillations and physics beyond standard assumptions.

Project 1: Quantum-gravity corrections to neutrino entanglement. Investigated Planck-scale quantum-gravity corrections to neutrino mass-squared splittings and mixing angles, and their effect on the von Neumann entanglement entropy of two-flavor oscillations. Outcome: published in Nuclear Physics B (2026).

Project 2: Neutrino oscillations in matter and quantum entanglement. Studied matter effects on two-flavor oscillations using the von Neumann entropy of the reduced flavor density matrix, comparing entanglement evolution in matter with that in vacuum. Outcome: published in the International Journal of Quantum Information.

Project 3: Quantum Fisher information and sterile neutrinos. Applied quantum and classical Fisher information (QFI, CFI) to sterile-neutrino MSW and non-standard-interaction (NSI) scenarios, investigated exact QFI–CFI saturation for an NSI parameter, and compared the sensitivity of KM3NeT and IceCube geometries using the Cramér–Rao bound. Outcome: preprint arXiv:2604.01256, under review.

September 2026 – Present

Collaborative Research Project

Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, India

Collaborator: Dristii Patel, B.S. student

This ongoing project studies the effects of Earth's density profile on neutrino oscillations, and neutrino asymmetries, using numerical methods.

  • Developed Python programs for neutrino propagation through Earth's layered density profile using the Preliminary Reference Earth Model (PREM).
  • Studied how variations in matter density affect oscillations.
  • Explored neutrino asymmetries and symmetry-violation studies, including CP, CPT and T.
14 – 24 July 2026

Mini Project — 10th Vietnam School on Neutrinos (VSoN10)

International Center for Interdisciplinary Science and Education (ICISE), Quy Nhon, Vietnam

Supervisor: Dr. Jake McKean

Project: Neutrino Energy Reconstruction Using Lepton Kinematics vs. Hadron Information.

  • Used NEUT to simulate charged-current quasi-elastic (CCQE) and non-CCQE interaction events and generate energy datasets.
  • Reconstructed neutrino energy with the lepton-kinematic method.
  • Compared reconstructed and true energies to quantify the effects of final-state interactions and nuclear binding energy.

Research focus: Neutrino energy reconstruction, Monte Carlo simulation, interaction kinematics, experimental neutrino physics.
